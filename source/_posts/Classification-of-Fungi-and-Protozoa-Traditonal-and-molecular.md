Traditionally, the main differences between fungi and protozoa are as follows:

+ Fungi
	+ mainly multicellular eukaryotic organisms
	+ normally saprophytes means they feed on dead and decay organic matter
	+ the same ancestor
+ Protozoa
	+ unicellular eukaryotic organisms
	+ could be autotrophic or heterotrophic

<p>But in the view of molecular biology, these nomenclature critirias are somewhat unprecise. For example, <b><em>Enterocytozoon bieneusi</em></b>, which was able to infect humans, especially immune deficient people like HIV patients and organ tranplatated individuals, is a major zoonotic microsporidia.</p><p>A few years ago, this kind of organism was treated as protozoa, while the sequencing results curve a crapses on the conclusion. Based on the nonmenclature analysis, microsporidia was classified as <b><em>fungi</em></b>.</p><p><b><em>Cell</em></b> publised a paper in the last week, it said that the process of PCR relplication is random, but the assemble of double strand is very percise.

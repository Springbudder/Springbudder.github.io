```
What is Medicare Clinical Lab Fee Schedule?
```

> The Medicare Clinical Laboratory Fee Schedule (CLFS) is a payment system used by the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) to reimburse clinical laboratories for their services. The CLFS sets the payment rates for laboratory tests that are performed on specimens collected from Medicare beneficiaries. The payment rates are based on the national limitation amount, which is determined by CMS through a public process. The CLFS payment rates are adjusted annually for inflation, and may also be adjusted based on changes in technology, costs, and other factors. The CLFS applies to a wide range of laboratory tests, including blood tests, urinalysis, microbiology, and genetic testing. Clinical laboratories must bill Medicare for these services using the appropriate codes and payment rates listed on the CLFS.

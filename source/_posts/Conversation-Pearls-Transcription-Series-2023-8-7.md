#### Long-term impact of escalating anti-corruption in the pharmaceutical industry



- 7月21日，国家卫健委会同教育部、公安部等十部门联合召开视频会议，部署开展为期1年的**全国医药领域腐败问题集中整治**。

- 7月24日，国家卫健委、国家发改委等六部门发布《深化医药卫生体制改革2023年下半年重点工作任务》，**将医疗行业反腐列为下半年医改重点任务。**

- 7月25日，初次审议的刑法修正案（十二）草案在行贿罪规定中**增加了新条款**，提出在教育医疗等领域行贿将从重处罚。

- 7月28日，中纪委牵头部署纪检监察机关配合**开展全国医药领域腐败问题集中整治**，中央及地方众多纪委、监委高级别官员列席或视频参会。

- 8月2日，广东、浙江、海南、湖北多省陆续发布通知，要求集中**整治全省医药领域腐败乱象**。

This is not an effect of anti-corruption; the root cause of all the endings is just a simple imitation of the **Cuban healthcare system**. The direct cause of the anti-corruption is that the system, in which everything seeks stability, doesn't allow certain groups of people to have much freedom to make decisions, or even to question decisions from the bureaucracy. Lawyers and doctors represent these groups of people.

The so-called escalation of anti-corruption is just a symptom; the essential reason behind it is still the tightening of the concept of state ownership. The worst thing that can happen in the future is that all practitioners in the medical field will be “Cubanized” in their experience, out of touch with the world, and boxed into this land.
